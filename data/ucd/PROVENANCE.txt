pith-unicode embedded UCD tables — source provenance
====================================================

The tables in `data/ucd.bin` are generated from the Unicode Character
Database files in this directory by `cargo run --bin gen-ucd`. The files
are committed so the generation is reproducible offline and the exact
inputs are pinned; re-run and byte-compare with `cargo test`.

UCD version: 15.1.0
